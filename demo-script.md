# 🎤 Demo Script: 2-Minute Presentation

**Presenter:** Muhammad Faiq Naveed

This script matches **Presentation Mode** in the app, step by step.
Each topic has **8 steps** of about **15 seconds** each, **2 minutes in total**. You present every step.

**How to run the demo**
1. Open `index.html` and click your topic, then **▶ Start**.
2. Click **🎤 Presentation Mode** in the top bar.
3. Read the lines for each step, then press **Next ▶** (or the → arrow key / a presentation clicker).
4. After step 8, **Next** opens the **Quiz** for the audience. Finish on **⭐ Credits**.

> Tip: practise with a stopwatch. Aim for about 15 seconds per step (roughly 30–35 words). Look at the audience, not the screen, and point at the 3D model while it moves.

### 🗣️ What to say

**Step 1: Welcome to Sky Shield** *(0:00)*
> "Assalamu alaikum! Our model is Sky Shield. This is the Earth, surrounded by transparent shells. Each shell is a layer of the atmosphere. The layers are enlarged so you can see them."
>
> *Then press **Next ▶**.*

**Step 2: The verse: a protected ceiling** *(0:15)*
> "In Surah Al-Anbiya 21:32, Allah says: 'And We made the sky a protected ceiling, but they, from its signs, are turning away.' Now let's look at the science separately."
>
> *Then press **Next ▶**.*

**Step 3: Troposphere: where weather happens** *(0:30)*
> "The lowest layer is the troposphere, up to about 12 km. Almost all clouds, rain and storms happen here. It holds most of the air we breathe."
>
> *Then press **Next ▶**.*

**Step 4: Ozone blocks harmful UV** *(0:45)*
> "These purple rays are ultraviolet light from the Sun. The ozone layer in the stratosphere absorbs all UV-C and most UV-B. Watch what happens when I switch the ozone off: the rays reach the ground."
>
> *Then press **Next ▶**.*

**Step 5: Meteor shower!** *(1:00)*
> "Now let's launch meteors. They hit the atmosphere at huge speed, glow, and burn up before they reach us. Dozens of tonnes of space dust enter every day."
>
> *Then press **Next ▶**.*

**Step 6: Mesosphere: the meteor shield** *(1:15)*
> "Most meteors burn up in the mesosphere, about 50 to 85 km up. It is the coldest layer, around minus 90 degrees Celsius at the top."
>
> *Then press **Next ▶**.*

**Step 7: Thermosphere, exosphere and 86:11** *(1:30)*
> "Higher up are the thermosphere, with auroras and the Space Station, and the exosphere, which fades into space. In 86:11 Allah swears by 'the sky which returns', which scholars explained as returning rain."
>
> *Then press **Next ▶**.*

**Step 8: Real life & reflection** *(1:45)*
> "So the next time you check the UV index or see a shooting star, remember the protected ceiling above us. Now let's try a quick quiz. Thank you!"
>
> *Then press **Next ▶**.*

**After step 8:** press **Start Quiz ▶** and ask the audience the 5 quiz questions.
